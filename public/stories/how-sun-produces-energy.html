How Does the Sun Produce Energy?

GpSpace • Space & science explainer

The Sun’s core is under enormous pressure and temperature. In the dominant proton–proton chain, hydrogen nuclei ultimately combine through a sequence of reactions that produces helium and releases energy.

What should you know?

That energy moves outward through the Sun and is eventually emitted as sunlight and other electromagnetic radiation.

Sunlight takes about eight minutes to travel from the Sun to Earth, allowing the energy from our star to power Earth’s climate and ecosystems.
