What Is a Lagrange Point?

GpSpace • Space & science explainer

In the simplified Sun–Earth system there are five classical Lagrange points. Spacecraft can use these regions to reduce some station-keeping requirements or to obtain useful observing geometries.

What should you know?

Sun–Earth L2 lies roughly 1.5 million kilometres from Earth on the side away from the Sun. Space observatories can operate near this region while keeping the Sun, Earth and Moon generally in a convenient direction for thermal control.

Lagrange points are not magical stationary spots. Real missions orbit around or move near these regions and make periodic corrections.
