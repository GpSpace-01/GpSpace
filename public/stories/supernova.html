What Is a Supernova?

GpSpace • Space & science explainer

Some massive stars end their lives when their cores collapse, triggering powerful explosions. Other supernovae arise from white-dwarf systems in which thermonuclear processes run out of control.

What should you know?

Supernovae can forge and distribute many heavy elements and can leave behind neutron stars or black holes in some core-collapse cases.

Astronomers study supernovae to understand stellar evolution, chemical enrichment and, in some cases, the expansion history of the universe.
