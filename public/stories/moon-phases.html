Why Does the Moon Have Phases?

GpSpace • Space & science explainer

The Moon does not produce its own visible sunlight. Half of it is illuminated by the Sun at any moment, but the amount of that illuminated half visible from Earth changes during its orbit.

What should you know?

The main phases are new moon, first quarter, full moon and last quarter, with crescent and gibbous phases between them.

The phases are caused by geometry, not by Earth’s shadow. Earth’s shadow is involved during a lunar eclipse, which is a different event.
