Our Solar System: A Quick Guide

GpSpace • Astronomy guide

Our Solar System is centered on the Sun, a star that contains almost all of the system's mass. Eight planets orbit the Sun, along with dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, comets, dust and other small bodies.

Four rocky worlds

Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars are terrestrial planets. They have solid surfaces and are relatively small compared with the outer planets.

The giant planets

Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants dominated by hydrogen and helium. Uranus and Neptune are ice giants with interiors containing substantial amounts of water, ammonia and methane in high-pressure forms.

Beyond Neptune

The outer Solar System contains icy bodies and dwarf planets. Pluto is one well-known example. The Kuiper Belt is a broad region containing many small icy objects beyond Neptune.
