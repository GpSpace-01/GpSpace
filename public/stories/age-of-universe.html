How Old Is the Universe?

GpSpace • Space & science explainer

Scientists estimate the age of the universe using measurements such as cosmic expansion and the cosmic microwave background. Different observations constrain the parameters of cosmological models.

What should you know?

The age is not measured by simply finding the oldest visible star. Instead, observations are combined within models that describe how the universe expanded and evolved.

Because light travels at a finite speed, telescopes also allow astronomers to observe earlier stages of cosmic history by looking farther away.
