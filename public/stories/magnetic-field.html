Earth's Magnetic Field: Our Invisible Shield

GpSpace • Earth & space guide

Earth's magnetic field is generated mainly by moving electrically conducting material in the liquid outer core. This process, called the geodynamo, creates a magnetic environment extending far into space.

Why does it matter?

The field helps deflect many charged particles from the solar wind. Earth's atmosphere and magnetic environment together reduce the amount of energetic solar and cosmic radiation reaching the surface.

Do the magnetic poles move?

Yes. The locations of the magnetic poles change over time because the magnetic field is dynamic. A moving magnetic pole is not the same thing as an imminent magnetic reversal.

Could space weather affect technology?

Strong solar storms can disturb Earth's magnetosphere and ionosphere. Depending on the event, they can affect satellite operations, radio communication, navigation systems and electrical infrastructure. Monitoring space weather helps operators prepare for these effects.
