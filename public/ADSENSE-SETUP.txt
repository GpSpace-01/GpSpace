GpSpace V3 — AdSense setup

This package is prepared for AdSense but does NOT contain a fake publisher ID or active ads.

When Google AdSense approves the site:
1. In AdSense, add your site and complete verification.
2. Copy Google's official AdSense code using your own publisher ID.
3. Add it to the <head> of index.html and any pages where Google instructs you to place it.
4. Replace the visible ADVERTISEMENT placeholder areas with Google's approved ad units if you choose manual placements, or use Auto ads.
5. Keep the Privacy Policy and other site information accurate and update them if your data/advertising setup changes.

Important: approval is not guaranteed. Google evaluates the entire site and requires original, useful content and compliance with its policies.
