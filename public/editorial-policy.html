GPSPACE EDITORIAL POLICY

How GpSpace creates and updates content

This page explains the principles used for GpSpace stories, videos, current-news pages and community questions.

Original explanations

GpSpace aims to publish original explanations rather than copying articles from other websites. External material may be quoted or summarized only as needed to explain a topic, with links to the source where appropriate.

Sources

For current mission information and scientific claims, GpSpace may consult primary sources such as NASA, ESA and NASA JPL, along with other established scientific sources. Source links are provided when they materially help readers verify a claim or continue their research.

News feeds

The Live News section may retrieve headlines and short descriptions from public feeds. Third-party headlines remain attributable to their original publisher. GpSpace's context is intended to help readers understand the topic, while the original source remains the place to read the full report.

Videos

GpSpace videos are published on the official GpSpace YouTube channel. The website may embed those videos. When a dedicated watch page exists, it includes additional written context and source information so the page is useful even beyond the embedded player.

Uncertainty and corrections

Science changes as new observations and analyses become available. GpSpace tries to distinguish established evidence from interpretation and open questions. If a factual error is identified, readers can contact GpSpace with the page and claim involved so it can be reviewed.

Community questions

User-submitted questions are moderated. GpSpace may publish selected questions and answers when they are appropriate for a general audience and useful to other readers.
