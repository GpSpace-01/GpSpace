Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026.

GpSpace respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the GpSpace website and how third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. GpSpace is an independent space and science information website connected to the GpSpace YouTube channel.

Information we may receive

When you visit the website, technical information may be processed by the hosting platform or other services, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location, IP-related information, pages viewed, referring page and basic request information. GpSpace does not intentionally ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information simply to read public pages.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for site operation, performance measurement, security or advertising. Third-party services may place or access cookies or similar identifiers subject to their own policies and the choices available to you.

Google AdSense and advertising

If GpSpace is approved for and enables Google AdSense or another Google advertising product, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to store or access information on a device, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, limit repeated advertising and, where permitted and selected, personalise advertisements based on information such as browsing activity.

Google may use advertising cookies to help serve ads based on a user's visits to this and other websites. Advertising personalisation and consent requirements can vary by region. Where Google requires a consent mechanism, GpSpace may use Google's available consent-management features or another compliant solution to collect and communicate the visitor's choices.

Users can review or manage available Google advertising and privacy choices through Google's own privacy and advertising controls. Third-party advertising providers may have their own privacy policies and opt-out mechanisms. GpSpace does not control how independent third parties process information under their own services.

European and other regional consent

Visitors from regions where consent is required for cookies or personalised advertising may be shown a consent or privacy message when advertising services are active. The availability and wording of such messages can depend on the visitor's region and the services enabled on the website. GpSpace will use applicable Google consent-management functionality when required for its advertising configuration.

Analytics and measurement

If analytics or measurement tools are enabled, they may collect information about how visitors use the website, such as page views, traffic sources, device information and approximate geographic information. These services help GpSpace understand which pages are useful and improve the website.

YouTube and external links

GpSpace links to YouTube and may link to NASA, ESA, NASA JPL, CNEOS and other external sources. When you follow an external link, the destination website's own privacy policy and terms apply. Embedded or linked services may collect information according to their own settings and policies.

Live News

The Live News section retrieves current headlines from external public feeds. GpSpace may display a headline, short description, source name and link to the original report. GpSpace does not claim ownership of third-party source content and encourages readers to consult the original source for the complete report.

Children

GpSpace is a general-audience educational website. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe a child has submitted personal information may contact GpSpace at the email address below.

Data retention and security

GpSpace does not operate a user-account system on this website. Information handled by hosting, analytics, advertising or external services may be retained according to those providers' policies. No method of internet transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where presented, through consent or privacy controls provided on the website. Blocking some cookies may affect certain website functions or advertising. You can also choose not to follow external links or interact with embedded third-party services.

Contact

For privacy questions or corrections, contact gpspace.one@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated when the website, advertising configuration or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
