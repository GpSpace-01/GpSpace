GpSpace V13.1 video-page system

Every GpSpace YouTube upload now gets an indexable dedicated page automatically:
https://gpspace.site/videos/watch/VIDEO_ID

A companion guide route is also available:
https://gpspace.site/stories/video/VIDEO_ID

The Cloudflare Worker fetches the YouTube title at request time, creates a VideoObject JSON-LD block, embeds the video, and adds original GpSpace context. The sitemap route dynamically includes the current rolling video cycle plus the pinned historical videos supplied for this release.

No manual page creation is required for future uploads. The Videos hub links each video to its dedicated page.
