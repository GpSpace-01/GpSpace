HOW GPSPACE WORKS

Original explanations, clearly sourced.

GpSpace uses its own explanations to add context to space-science information, while linking readers to primary or established sources when a topic depends on current mission facts.

Original content

Our stories explain concepts in plain language and focus on what the evidence means, what scientists know, and what remains uncertain. Embedded videos are supplementary rather than the only content on a page.

Sources and updates

For current missions and discoveries, GpSpace checks source material from organisations such as NASA, ESA and NASA JPL and links readers to the original report where appropriate.

Corrections welcome

If you spot an error, tell us what page or claim needs correction. GpSpace can review and update published explanations as new evidence becomes available.

Black Holes

Black holes are regions where gravity is strong enough that an event horizon forms. Astronomers do not photograph the inside of a black hole; they infer black holes from their effects on nearby matter, light and stars.

Solar Activity & Space Weather

The Sun constantly releases particles and electromagnetic radiation. Flares and coronal mass ejections can produce space-weather effects, making solar physics relevant to satellites, radio systems and other technology.
