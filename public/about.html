ABOUT GPSPACE

Explore • Learn • Inspire

GpSpace is an independent space and astronomy media project creating accessible explanations, original videos and current-space updates about the universe, missions, astronomy, planetary science and the science connecting space with life on Earth.

Our purpose

Our goal is to make fascinating science easier to understand without turning uncertainty into certainty. GpSpace separates established observations from interpretation, open questions and speculation.

The site combines original evergreen explainers with current-news links and a video library connected to the official GpSpace YouTube channel.

How we build stories

For evergreen topics, GpSpace explains the underlying science in its own words. For current missions and discoveries, we consult primary or established scientific sources and link readers to the original reporting when practical.

When a page includes an embedded YouTube video, the written material is intended to add context, definitions, background and source information rather than simply reproduce the video.

EDITORIAL VALUES

Clear. Curious. Evidence-aware.

  • Explain technical ideas in accessible language.
  • Distinguish measurements from interpretation.
  • Link to primary sources for important current facts.
  • Correct material errors when they are identified.
  • Respect the rights and terms of external sources.
