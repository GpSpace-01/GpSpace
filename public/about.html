ABOUT GPSPACE

Explore • Learn • Inspire

GpSpace is a space and astronomy media brand creating accessible stories, videos and updates about the universe, space missions, astronomy, planetary science and the science that connects space with life on Earth.

Our purpose

Our goal is simple: make fascinating science easier to understand while encouraging curiosity, evidence-based thinking and a deeper interest in the universe.

GpSpace combines original explainers and videos with a live news section that points readers toward current reporting from established space-science sources.
